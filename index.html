How I work

Clear scope, controlled delivery

Business requirements, technical decisions, and project ownership stay visible from the first workshop through handover.

01

Discover

Workshops with the people who run the operation map the current process, surface the real pain points, and set measurable priorities.

02

Design

Requirements are mapped against standard Odoo capabilities to shape the target process, with configuration, integration, and customization decisions made explicit.

03

Implement

Configuration, development, data migration, infrastructure, and testing move through controlled phases, with progress and risks tracked and visible throughout.

04

Handover

UAT sign-off, training, documentation, cutover, and support close out the engagement, so the operating team owns the system from go-live onward.
