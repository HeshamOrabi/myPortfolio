Story

Ten years across business and IT

A software engineering graduate with ten years of experience across Egypt and Saudi Arabia, I start Odoo work with the business process: how people sell, buy, manufacture, move stock, and report — then configure the system around that reality.

ERP delivery stays paired with hands-on technical work: Odoo 14 through 19, data and integrations, Linux, Docker, cloud deployments, and the applications and websites built around the core system.

Infrastructure planning and BOQ/tender preparation for offices and buildings round out the work, including field oversight of IT infrastructure for Saudi Ministry of Tourism locations in Makkah and Madinah.
