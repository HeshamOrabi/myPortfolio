Privacy notice

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and contact actions. The analytics script loads automatically when you open the site.

What is measured

Analytics is limited to page views, section views, scroll depth, navigation, language switches, and clicks on email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and other professional-profile links. These measurements help evaluate whether the portfolio is useful and whether visitors can reach the relevant contact options.

What is not collected

The site does not send message contents, email addresses entered by visitors, passwords, or other sensitive personal information to analytics. There is no contact form, account, advertising profile, or sale of personal data.

Analytics cookies

Google Analytics may set first-party measurement cookies such as _ga. They are configured for secure transport, SameSite protection, and a maximum lifetime of 90 days. These cookies cannot read contact messages because the site has no form.

Third-party processing

Measurement data is processed by Google under its privacy policy. Advertising signals and ad-personalization signals are disabled in this implementation.
